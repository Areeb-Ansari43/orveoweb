FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a website take?

Most Starter websites are live within 7 days of receiving your details. Premium websites take around 10 to 14 days.

Do I have to pay the £20 a month?

It's optional. It covers hosting, security, backups and updates to your content. Without it, we hand the website over to you to host yourself.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimisation: structuring your website so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers understand your business and recommend it.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once it's paid for, the design, content and domain are yours.

I don't have photos or text. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We write the text for you and can use high-quality licensed photos until you have your own.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We'll keep what works, fix what doesn't, and make sure you don't lose your Google rankings in the move.
