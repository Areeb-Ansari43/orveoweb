Questions

Not sure yet? Fair enough.

I get most of my work by word of mouth. Do I need a website?

Word of mouth is great, but most people still look you up before they call. A good website backs up the recommendation, shows your work and makes it easy to get in touch, so fewer of those leads go to someone else.

I'm not good with tech. How much do I need to do?

Very little. I write the text, find the images and set everything up. You just answer a few questions about your business and tell me what you'd like changed.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every website is built for phones first, then tablets and desktops, because that's where most of your customers will find you.

Will I show up on Google?

Every package includes SEO basics like page titles, descriptions and a sitemap. Professional and Premium go further, with Google Business Profile setup, Search Console and advanced SEO. Premium adds a 4-week SEO improvement programme.

What if I already have a website?

I can redesign it (from £150) or rebuild it as part of a package, keeping what works and making sure you don't lose your place on Google.

What happens after launch?

Either you take your code and host it yourself, or I look after hosting, security and small updates for £25 a month. It's up to you.
